# Placeholder image credits

These files fill photo slots until real portraits, member works, or official
event photos are provided. They must not be read as finished attributions.

## Default artwork

`/images/default-artwork.jpg` is the site-wide default artwork and cover
image. It is 陳嬋娟's 膠彩 painting of black-faced spoonbills (黑面琵鷺),
signed 嬋娟, provided by Jason for this site. On the site its alt text is
「黑面琵鷺（陳嬋娟 膠彩）」. Missing artwork and cover slots fall back to
this file.

The earlier `placeholder-artwork-01.jpg`–`08.jpg`,
`placeholder-exhibition-hero.jpg`, and `placeholder-news-01.jpg` /
`placeholder-news-02.jpg` files (copied from this project's
`public/gallery/` folder) have been replaced by this default image.

## Portraits

`placeholder-portrait-01.svg` and `placeholder-portrait-02.svg` are original
neutral silhouettes drawn for this site. They are not photographs of any
person and were not downloaded from Wikimedia or any other third party.

No Wikimedia Commons images were used.

## Gallery paintings used as placeholders

Site artwork slots (home featured works, exhibition covers and galleries,
news covers, and member work galleries) use the existing photographs in
`public/gallery/Painting/`. Filenames are Facebook export IDs and do not
identify the artist, so captions are 「作品」（作者【待補】） / "Work (artist [TBD])"
and are not formal attributions. `/images/default-artwork.jpg` remains the
fallback when a work or cover is missing.
